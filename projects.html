01 / SAAS STARTUP

BUILDING MY KIRANA

A software which helps local grocery stores to manage their customers, reduce crowd bottlenecks, and make everyday neighborhood ordering fast and organized.

Founder · Dev · Marketer Next.js · Supabase ● In Progress
Live App ↗ GitHub: Private
THE IDEA Move the everyday neighborhood kirana-shop flow — browsing, ordering, waiting — into a lightning-fast digital queue.
THE PROBLEM Customers crowd small grocery shops and wait around for simple items, while shop owners juggle pen-and-paper orders with no easy management system.
THE APPROACH Building solo as founder and developer — creating both the customer mobile ordering experience and the shopkeeper order fulfillment dashboard to help stores manage their customers.
THE BUILD Engineered end-to-end on Next.js, integrated with cloud backend, deployed and actively iterated with real shopkeepers.
STATUS Active alpha development — live testing and feature expansion underway.
